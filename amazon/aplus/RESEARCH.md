# A+ research: glass / kundan bangles on amazon.in (checked 2026-10-17)

Method: searched amazon.in for "kundan glass bangles set of 4 women" (940 results), fetched the top 24 listings and measured their A+ section, then opened the interesting ones.

## What the market looks like
- Only about 40% of the top listings have real A+ content. The rest (including STYOLLO and most generics) show a plain text description.
- NMII, the biggest seller in the category (about 728 reviews on its top listing), has **no real A+ on its lead listing**, just a text block. Some NMII variants do have a banner.
- Everything is priced low and heavily discounted (70-95% off MRP). Nobody sells "premium".
- Our own live listings (the other KSG / KMS / GVK colours) have no A+ yet.

## What the brands that do it are doing
| Brand | A+ pattern | Takeaway |

| ZAYA (4.8 stars, set of 4 kundan) | 2 full-width 970x600 banners: logo, headline, 4 icon+benefit rows (quality, craft, finish, lightweight), a "handmade with love" tagline and a trust strip, then a 5-product comparison table | Closest competitor in look. Clean, gold/cream, benefit icons. Good structure to beat. |
| GIVA (premium silver) | 970x300 brand logo banner, 970x600 image + paragraph modules, 970x300 closer | Brand-first storytelling, big photography, little text. |
| Peora | "From the brand" carousel (Brand Story): logo card, model photo, shop-by-category cards | Cross-sells the range. Needs the Brand Story type. |
| NMII | text only on lead listing | Opening for us. |

## What works / what does not
Works: one hero with a clear promise, benefit icons (not paragraphs), real colour range shown on one image, a size/fit guide (bangles are returned for size), occasion photography, a range/cross-sell strip, brand consistency.
Does not: repeating the same studio shots that are already in the gallery, long paragraphs baked into images, small text (mobile shows 970px at about 375px wide), price/offer/guarantee wording (rejected), colour-specific hero on a document shared by many colours.

## Plan for Bangle Bliss (one global document per design, applied to the whole parent)
1. Hero 970x600: new Magic scene (emerald bangles on cream silk) + product promise + 4 facts strip.
2. Four-up 220x220: four NEW Magic occasion scenes (Diwali diya, velvet tray, bridal lehenga, haldi), each a different colour so the range shows.
3. Colour wall 970x600: all 13 real studio shots with names (real photos on purpose: Magic can shift colours).
4. Size guide 970x600: how to choose + inches/cm table.
5. Range strip 970x300 (Image & Dark Text Overlay): the three designs, cross-sell.

Same recipe later for KMS and GVK: change the spec and the scene colours.
